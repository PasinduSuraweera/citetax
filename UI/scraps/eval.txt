Interface Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind

Responsible AI
A privacy layer in front of everything
A local model checks anything a user sends before it goes further. It strips personal details such as TIN, NIC and employer name, and
blocks wrong or sensitive content from reaching the hosted model. Nothing private leaves the machine.
No made up figures
The model never touches the arithmetic. Every step has a rule behind it.
No outdated law
Retrieval is filtered by year, and the citation shows the dates it applied.
No advice
Out of scope and advisory questions are refused with a reason.
Nothing hidden
The user can open every step and every source, and check the answer.
